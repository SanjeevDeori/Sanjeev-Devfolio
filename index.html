The quiet space between

I’m drawn to the quiet space between a question and its answer, where messy data becomes a model, and a model becomes something a person can hold.

A pipeline is patience.
A model is a guess that learns.
An interface is trust.

I like working along that whole line, because the interesting problems rarely respect the borders between data, models and interfaces. Curiosity pulls me across disciplines, and somewhere between exploration and experimentation, abstract ideas begin to take shape into something meaningful.
